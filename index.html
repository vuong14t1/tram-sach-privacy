Privacy Policy – Tram Sach Uploader

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tram Sach Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Trạm Sách Cuối Ngày (@tramsachcuoingay) to upload the owner's own videos through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

  • The tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's consent through Google OAuth (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube).
  • It uploads the owner's video files and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share data of any other person.

Storage and revocation

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
